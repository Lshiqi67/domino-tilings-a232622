# 基本块证明路线图解 / Illustrated guide to the block proof

**Shiqi Liu** · 2026-10-25

两份讲义各 10 页、6 组示意图，按相同结构解释三行多米诺铺法的基本块证明路线：

1. 题面、矩形对称群，以及 Burnside 引理的双重计数证明。
2. 切口状态、完整转移表、首次返回路径与基本块分类。
3. 唯一分解，以及上下反射、左右反射、半圈旋转在块串上的作用。
4. 生成函数 F、D、H、V、R 的组合含义，尤其是 D(x²) 与 D(x)² 的区别。
5. 合并四类不动点，推导主生成函数、初值和七阶递推。

Each edition contains 10 pages and six diagram groups, with matching organization. They explain the symmetry group and Burnside's lemma, cut states and first-return blocks, unique decomposition, the symmetry action on words, the combinatorial meaning of the generating functions, and the final rational formula and recurrence. Special attention is given to why a forced reflected copy contributes D(x²), rather than D(x)².

这些材料是对论文基本块路线的教学整理，不包含新的研究结论。示意图按骨牌坐标绘制，颜色仅区分方向。讲义由 AI 辅助整理与排版；数值、代数和排版核对不等于独立数学审阅或该路线的完整形式化验证。

These are explanatory notes for the manuscript's block proof, not new research results. Diagrams follow the defining domino coordinates; colors indicate orientation only. The notes were prepared with AI assistance. Numerical, algebraic, and layout checks do not constitute independent mathematical review or complete formal verification of this route.
