\begin{abstract}
We study domino tilings of odd-height rectangles of even width up to rectangle symmetries, with particular attention to the generating-function conjecture recorded as OEIS A232622. This working draft presents candidate derivations using indecomposable blocks and half-rectangle boundary states. It also records proposed stabilizer formulas, asymptotic calculations, an odd-height transfer expression, and a five-row polynomial certificate. The arguments and exposition remain subject to correction and revision. This work is unfinished, and no solution of the conjecture is claimed. Lean formalization and verification are still required; the computational checks reported here do not establish the correctness of the unbounded-parameter arguments.
\end{abstract}
