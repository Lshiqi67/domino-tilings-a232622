We count unlabelled domino tilings of odd-height rectangles of even width up to rectangle symmetries. For three rows, an indecomposable-block decomposition and a half-rectangle construction give two proofs of the generating function recorded in OEIS A232622, together with its seventh-order recurrence and initial values. We determine the exact stabilizer classes and the asymptotic proportion of symmetric tilings. For arbitrary fixed odd height, we give a transfer-matrix expression with explicit symmetry boundary conditions; at height five, a six-dimensional polynomial certificate yields an explicit rational generating function and a fourteenth-order recurrence. A public Lean 4 formalization covers both three-row proofs, the stabilizer formulas, the odd-height counting and rationality results, the five-row certificate and recurrence, and the three-row asymptotics. The formal counts are defined from actual tilings and their orbits.
